How wood moves

Matching the reference: the plank travels along the machine bed (long axis), through rollers, into the circular saw — not into the broadside. Sawdust concentrates at the blade. Beyond the outfeed end, the explored sequence appears: blanks → mortise & tenon → chair frame.

  • Registration on rollers
  • Cut at the blade (Daibutsuyō force)
  • Kerf + airborne dust

  • Assembly density (Zenshūyō)
